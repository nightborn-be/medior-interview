## Read this first

**This part is written without an AI assistant.** It is the only one in the submission.
Everywhere else we encourage you to use one and we look at how you use it. Here we want your
own words.

This is not a moral rule, it is a practical one. We will come back to each of your answers
during the interview, and we will systematically go one level below what you wrote: the exact
number, the name of the tool, what happened that day. Text you did not write yourself does not
survive a minute of that conversation. You gain nothing and you lose a lot.

**We do not judge style or spelling.** A spell checker, or a translation if French or English is
not your working language, are no problem at all. Having a model write or rewrite the substance
is.

Only write down things you can go into out loud.
