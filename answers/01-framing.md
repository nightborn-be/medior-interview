# Part A. Framing

*About 20 minutes. Keep it short: one page is plenty.*

## 1. The real business problem

*In two sentences. Not the client's request, the problem behind it.*



## 2. The number I want to move

*Just one. Roughly what it is today, and how you estimate it. If you need an assumption to get
to a number, write the assumption down.*



## 3. What I still do not know

*The call notes do not answer everything. What is still missing that would change your
recommendation if it landed the wrong way, and how would you go and find out?*

*We are looking for a decision-changing unknown: if it lands badly, you change your mind. Not a
list of accesses to request.*
